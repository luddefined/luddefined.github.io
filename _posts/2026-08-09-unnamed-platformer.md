This platformer was made during the second semester of my second year in university. It's also my second Unreal Engine 5 project, and the second platformer assignment our course has given us. A third person action platformer was on the specification, which led me to take inspiration from first person action platformer *Neon White* for a platformer with card abilities, and a focus on speedrunning. 

Throughout development I learnt a lot about how much you can do with UE5 blueprints, which was quite a surprise, though I can't say with certainty that it's the pleasant type. Hidden amongst the course's demands were very detailed accounts for the level design reasoning, which I still try to justify with the few brightly coloured eye catching cubes 'strategically placed throughout the level'. Level design is still a topic I have much to learn about, which i am eager to put to use in my future projects and games.

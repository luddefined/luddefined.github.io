This project was the final C++ assignment in my entire university course, completed during the second year. We had to make a top-down game using the simple fast multimedia library (SFML), and I somehow managed to conjure up this.

A personal goal throughout its development was to attempt to make some form of 'framework' for the game's content, in part inspired by other activities I was doing in Godot and my journey with learning Unity and Unreal Engine. Whereas other C++ projects had me throughly confused at many points, the focus on designing the framework ended up with a *relatively* painless development process. Finally I could understand what a pointer did without having to look it up each time I suspected it might be useful, and I could comprehend a few more keywords and symbols than in the previous project. Though I can now see a much better way to go about implementing that framework, I am very content with what I had created, though I really should not have cut the idea of momentum on movement.

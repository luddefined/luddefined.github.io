What time is it? *Tea Time...* I don't believe it. The inside joke that spawned the name of Tea Time, the third game jam project I've worked on, featuring the same two friends from [game jam #1]({% post_url 2026-08-09-worst-elf-ever %}) alongside two others: One helped with programming, and the other did all of our [music](https://retraace.bandcamp.com/album/tea-time-ep) ([shoutout retraace](https://retraace.bandcamp.com/)). The returning cast did the same jobs as before, with way more preparation and with (probably) way less stress. This was submitted as a part of my [university's game jam](https://itch.io/jam/uwe-games-starting-block-game-jam). Do not tell them that two of my team members were not studying at this univeristy at the time of this game jam.

I am fairly certain a lot of the design ideas of this game came from the highly reputable source of "vibes", alongside a little bit of influence from *Balatro*, with the whole rogue-like element. I also added a little bit of design inspiration from *Cookie Clicker*, in the sense that items are themed as stackable buildings.

One day I'd like to revisit this project, preferably alongside the other 4 people I worked on. I sense great potential in this game, where there are still many ideas and things we'd all like to include (or at least I am hoping the rest of them similarly possess unfinished business with this game). Sitting right next to "tell the player how the game works" and "more content", perhaps this could lean more into the shelved idea crafting specific teas for each customer, and I even had one idea of combining the rogue-like bit with some kind of idle gameplay. So many possibilities, and I'm hopeful that at least one of them reaches the light of day.

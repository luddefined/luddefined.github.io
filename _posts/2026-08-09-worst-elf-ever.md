**Original project date:** 18/12/23 - 26/12/23
{: .notice--info}

A submission for the [*Jame Gam Christmas Edition 2023*](https://itch.io/jam/jame-gam-christmas-edition-2023/rate/2440147), one *very* fondly remembered by the two other friends I worked with. One helped in programming while the other did all of the artwork, which we have all agreed as heavily carrying the quality of the game. This is the very first game project that any of us has completed (unless one of them secretly made something beforehand and is currently witholding that information from the rest of us); though we had all previously worked on concepts for an idea of various different games, this was the project that would break that cycle. It was the first time we had to deal with the whole "project management" and "game design" sectors of coming up with a game idea, and the consequential stress and crunch of game development that comes without the full mastery of those same skills. 

A symptom of first game ever made by us does come in the varied dubious design decisions you may stumble upon throughout the approximately 10 minutes of "very intense platforming". We have bootleg flappy bird that was added in likely due to the delerium caused by reusing the same red and white pole for our placeholder images. We also had to deal with the placement of a particular anvil, the 'special object' of this particular jam (and something we'd all prefix with 'accursed' and other similarly sinister synonyms). 

In spite of all the misery caused by everything going wrong a few hours before submission (wow, just like in the dev logs!) and with the taunting light of that wretched *checkpoint_on_brick.png*, it was still quite fun to work together with others to develop something, even if we definitely had our scope set far beyond what we could have hoped to achieve. Perhaps the true Christmas miracle was this project being delivered on time*.

(*and a few hours late, but they still accepted it!)
